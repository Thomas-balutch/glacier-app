Scientific context

Alpine glaciers are sensitive indicators of climate change. Since the end of the 19th century, glaciological observations have shown a widespread retreat of many glaciers in the Alpine massif.

This interactive map allows users to explore the location of several glaciers and access descriptive indicators such as average altitude, glacier surface area and the estimated evolution of their surface.

Currently, the database used in this observatory covers glaciers located in the French territory of the Alpine massif. A future extension may integrate other European Alpine glaciers.
