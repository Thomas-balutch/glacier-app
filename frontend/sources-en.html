Glacier data

The data used for glacier mapping and evolution come from international scientific databases.

  • GLIMS — Global Land Ice Measurements from Space
  • WGMS — World Glacier Monitoring Service
  • ESA Climate Change Initiative

Alpine wildlife data

Information about threatened alpine species is based on scientific literature and biodiversity conservation databases.

  • IUCN Red List
  • National Museum of Natural History
  • Alpine natural parks

Technologies used

  • HTML / CSS / JavaScript
  • Leaflet (interactive mapping)
  • Chart.js (data visualization)

Project objective

This website is an educational project aiming to make scientific data about Alpine glaciers accessible to the general public and to raise awareness of climate change impacts.
